## Residual scientific limits

- Noise, capacity saturation, drift, resolution change, approximate keys, learned forecasts, recurrent controls and natural-system efficacy were not tested. None is claimed.
- There is no functioning recent-history comparison. An improved comparator would be a separate extension with a new frozen protocol, not an edit of this outcome.
- Identifiability and donor-distance booleans cannot be reconstructed from the old saved scalars alone. The prepared raw-output worker must be executed under admitted resources, and its captured tensors independently checked. Source inspection is insufficient to close this gate.
- Three seeds quantify this construction's repeatability, not practical forecasting generalization or a powered population estimate.
- An external reviewer must assess originality/significance, the intervention estimand, shortcut counterexamples, information boundaries and the derivation. A separate operator must reproduce the exact released artifact. The release gates stay open until actual receipts exist.
