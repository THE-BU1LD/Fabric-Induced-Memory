# Research session entry point

Read `RESEARCH_STATE.json` and its referenced truth, protocol and development
records before research or engineering work. Existing frozen source identities,
historical artifacts, failures and negative outcomes remain authoritative.

Before finishing, append the actual changes, source identities, verification,
failures, decisions, limits and next executable action to the canonical index.
Do not present passing software tests as completed scientific evaluation or
change an existing protected outcome boundary without its required authorization.
