Publication readiness means a defensible contribution, correct claims, reproducible evidence, a finished manuscript, and a verified submission package. No checklist can guarantee acceptance or make a study scientifically perfect. Negative results are valid outcomes; improvement must never mean rerunning a closed study until it becomes positive.

This checklist records both completed and outstanding work; the execution disposition gives the current status of every item. It does not authorize spending, protected evaluation, upstream redistribution, or submission. An unchecked item closes only with its stated evidence. A route-specific item may be marked not applicable with a recorded reason, never silently removed.
